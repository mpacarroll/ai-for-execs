# Skill 6: widen along one axis at a time

**The rule:** after the first task works, there are exactly three ways to expand. Take
one at a time, or a regression cannot be attributed to anything.

## The three axes

- **Volume.** Same task, more of it. The safest, and it usually surfaces input variety
  your evaluation never saw.
- **Autonomy.** Same task, less human review. Only defensible once the confidently-wrong
  rate (skill 4) is actually measured, because that number is the whole justification.
- **Scope.** A new task. This is not an extension. It is a new definition of correct,
  and it starts again at skill 2.

## The trap

The moment of maximum temptation is right after the first success, because that is when
confidence peaks and scrutiny relaxes. The instinct is to widen all three axes at once.
That is also the setup for the first serious failure, and a serious failure after a
celebrated success is the one that sets a program back a year, because it spends
credibility rather than budget.

One axis. Measure. Then the next.
